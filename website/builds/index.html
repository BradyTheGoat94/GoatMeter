SET IT UP ONCE

Build the lines, then the macro.

  1. Open the skill window. Arrange each named line in the shown slot. Row 0 is the bottom slot and has the highest priority. The visual cards show the actual top-to-bottom arrangement.
  2. Open the game’s Macro editor. Add the shown hotbar lines as steps, in order. The Q/E labels here are examples; choose free slots and use those same slots in your macro.
  3. Use 10 ms by default, or test the optional 20 ms setting. Compare reliability and damage at your latency; neither setting guarantees optimal DPS. A delay is not your network ping.
  4. Bind a separate macro key in Settings → Key Settings → General → Macro. Hold it during the damage loop; keep the basic attack and manual abilities available. Mouse bindings may need AION 2 control mode.
  5. On a training target, confirm each line fires, your resources hold up, and charged attacks reach the intended stage. Test Skill Queue on and off; advice differs. Check more than one run.

These are configuration plans for the client’s own macro editor, not executable scripts or auto-import files. No third-party automation is needed.

Turn a setup into your setup.

01 · Keep the comparison fair

Use the same gear, target, buffs and fight length. Change one line or delay at a time. Repeat runs so crit variance does not decide the result.

02 · Watch what breaks

Look for skipped chains, interrupted charges, empty mana, lost rear uptime and buffs wasted during mechanics. A higher opener can still produce a worse fight.

03 · Compare selected-target damage

Use a completed GoatMeter fight export. Review the selected target and its observed duration. Adds and incomplete captures can distort a comparison.
